# Demo Video Checklist

Use this checklist when recording the required demonstration video.

1. Open VS Code and open the `world-happiness-analysis` folder.
2. Open the terminal.
3. Activate the virtual environment:
   `venv\Scripts\activate`
4. Run:
   `streamlit run app.py`
5. Show the Overview tab.
6. Change the year filter.
7. Select two or three countries and open Country Comparison.
8. Open Drivers and show the correlation chart.
9. Change the factor in the scatter plot.
10. Open Data and download the filtered CSV.
11. Stop the app and show the GitHub repository structure.

Suggested video length: 2–4 minutes.
